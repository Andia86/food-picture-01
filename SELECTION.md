# How entry.png was chosen

Four candidate food photographs were generated for this bounty and then
judged head-to-head on appetite appeal, lighting and realism (30 points in
all). The full judging notes are kept in `opus_food_judge.txt`.

| Candidate | Appeal | Lighting | Realism | Total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Ramen bowl, jammy yolk and chashu | 9 | 9 | 8 | **26** |
| Molten chocolate cake with ice cream | 9 | 9 | 7 | 25 |
| French seared duck breast | 8 | 8 | 9 | 25 |
| Mezze spread | 8 | 7 | 7 | 22 |

The ramen shot won: the glossy yolk, charred chashu, chili oil and rising
steam read as hot and ready to eat, and the side window light against the
dark room gives it the strongest contrast of the four. The dessert tied the
duck on points and has real punch, but its chocolate reads a little like a
CGI advert; the duck is the most realistic frame yet the least craveable;
the mezze table has no single main dish for the eye to land on.

`entry.png` in this repository is the winning ramen photograph, and
`main.py` publishes it unchanged.
